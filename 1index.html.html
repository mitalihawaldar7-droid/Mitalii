A little note for someone very special

Happy International
Men's Day Baba

Some things are so familiar that we forget to say them out loud. So today, I want to remind you how much you mean to me.

Thank you for being there in all the ways you have, for the things you do without being asked, for the sacrifices I may not always notice, and for loving me in your own quiet, unmistakable way.

I may not say it every day, but I am proud to be your daughter. I hope life is as kind to you as you have been to the people you love. You deserve good health, happiness, and a thousand little reasons to smile.

Love you, always. ♡
Made with all my love · 19 November
